# Native-speaker review

Review 20 fixed qualitative prompts plus at least 30 task-stratified samples.

For every response, record:

- `wolof_primary`: response is primarily Wolof.
- `fluency`: integer 1–5.
- `instruction_following`: integer 1–5.
- `factuality`: `pass`, `fail`, or `not_applicable`.
- `orthography`: `standard`, `acceptable_variant`, or `problem`.
- `unsafe`: whether the response is harmful.
- `notes`: free-text explanation.

Two reviewers should independently assess the hero candidate. Resolve disagreements before the publication gate. Do not replace this process with automatic language ID.
